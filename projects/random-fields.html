Research assistant project

Conditional spatial random fields for heterogeneous materials

Student research assistant · Chair of Computational Applied Mechanics, University of Wuppertal

PythonGSToolsGeostatisticsSpatial random fieldsConditional simulationUncertainty quantification
ToolsPython, GSTools
MethodsGeostatistics, variograms, conditional simulation
Used inMonte Carlo FEM

Motivation

Many engineering and natural materials are not uniform. Their local material properties vary from point to point, and these variations govern the local strain field and therefore the overall mechanical response. Because neighbouring points tend to have similar values, the variations are spatially correlated. Classical statistics cannot describe this structure, whereas geostatistics was developed for exactly this kind of data.

Approach

  • Measured property maps were normalised with a quantile transformation, so non-Gaussian data could be described by Gaussian random fields.
  • The spatial structure was captured with fitted variogram models.
  • Conditional realisations were generated: new, equally plausible property maps in which the distribution and spatial correlation of the real sample are preserved and the measured values are honoured.
  • The correlation between local material properties and local strain fields measured on the same specimens was quantified.

Outcome

Each realisation is treated as a virtual specimen. When many of them are fed into Monte Carlo finite element simulations with an in-house stochastic finite element code, the effect of natural material variability on the mechanical behaviour is shown and the resulting uncertainty is quantified.
